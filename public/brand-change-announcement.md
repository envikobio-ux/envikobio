# Brand Name Change Announcement

## Official Notice: Company Name Change

**Effective Date: July 10, 2026**

---

Dear Valued Customers, Partners, and Stakeholders,

We are pleased to announce that **Shanghai Enviko Biotechnology Co., Ltd.** has officially changed its English company name to **Shanghai Alvokor Biotechnology Co., Ltd.**

This change reflects our commitment to building a stronger, more recognizable global brand identity while maintaining our core values and dedication to delivering premium bio-synthetic raw materials for the cosmetic, pharmaceutical, and food industries.

---

## What Has Changed

| Item | Previous | Current |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Company Name (English)** | Shanghai Enviko Biotechnology Co., Ltd. | Shanghai Alvokor Biotechnology Co., Ltd. |
| **Brand Name** | Envikobio | Alvokorbio |
| **Website** | www.envikobio.com | www.alvokorbiosolution.com |
| **Email Domain** | @envikobio.com | @alvokorbio.com |

---

## What Remains the Same

- Our **Chinese company name** remains unchanged: 上海艾尔沃科尔生物技术有限公司
- Our **business operations, product quality, and service standards** remain unchanged
- Our **product portfolio, certifications, and technical capabilities** remain unchanged
- Our **existing contracts and agreements** remain fully valid and binding
- Our **team and management structure** remain unchanged

---

## Action Required

### For Customers & Partners

1. **Update your records** with our new company name and contact information
2. **Redirect future correspondence** to our new email addresses:
   - General inquiries: info@alvokorbio.com
   - Business development: BD@alvokorbio.com

**Transition Period: 3 months (July 10, 2026 - October 10, 2026)**

During this 3-month transition period:
