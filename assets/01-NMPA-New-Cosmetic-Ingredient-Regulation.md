## Why This Regulation Matters Now

On June 26, 2026, the National Medical Products Administration (NMPA) published Announcement No. 59 — the *Provisions for Management of New Cosmetic Ingredient Registration and Notification Dossiers* — effective **July 15, 2026**. This replaces the 2021 framework and represents the most significant regulatory shift for cosmetic ingredient supply chains in years.

For global buyers, formulators, and procurement teams, this isn't just a compliance update. It's a **supply chain restructuring event** that changes time-to-market, costs, and competitive dynamics across the entire cosmetic ingredients sector.

---

## The Three Core Changes

### 1. High-Risk Category Reduction: 10 → 5

The headline change: the "higher-risk functional claims" category has been **reduced from 10 to 5 categories**.

**Still requiring Registration (highest scrutiny):**

| Retained Category | Examples |
|-------------------|----------|
| Preservatives | Parabens, phenoxyethanol alternatives |
| Sunscreens | Novel UV filters |
| Colorants | New synthetic/natural dyes |
| Hair dyes | Oxidative dye precursors |
| Skin whitening agents | Novel depigmenting compounds |

**Now downgraded to Notification (simplified pathway):**

| Removed Category | Market Impact |
|------------------|--------------|
| Anti-hair loss agents | Faster entry for peptide-based scalp treatments |
| Anti-acne agents | Significant for nicotinamide, ectoine, salicylic acid alternatives |
| Anti-wrinkle agents | Opens door for GHK-Cu, HA peptides, retinol alternatives |
| Anti-dandruff agents | Simplified path for zinc pyrithione replacements |
| Deodorant agents | Easier entry for microbiome-friendly actives |

**What this means for your supply chain:** Ingredients previously requiring 12–24 months of registration review can now enter the market through notification in as little as **3–6 months**. For procurement teams, this translates to faster access to innovation ingredients and more supplier options.

### 2. Animal Alternative Testing Methods Accepted

The new regulation explicitly encourages **animal alternative testing** — including in vitro methods, computational toxicology, and AI-based safety assessment. This is critical for:

- **EU market alignment**: Products tested via alternative methods face fewer barriers under EU Cosmetics Regulation (EC 1223/2009), which has banned animal testing since 2013
- **Cross-market compliance**: A single safety data package can now serve both NMPA notification and EU CosIng registration requirements
- **Brand positioning**: Clean beauty brands can source ingredients with zero animal testing throughout the supply chain

**Alvokor BioSolution's compliance posture:** Our safety assessment dossiers for cosmetic-grade ingredients are compiled using internationally accepted alternative testing frameworks, ensuring compatibility with CosIng registration, FDA food additive reviews, and REACH exempt bio-intermediate classifications where applicable.

### 3. Administrative-Technical Separation

The previous regulation embedded detailed technical requirements (development reports, manufacturing processes, safety assessments) directly into the legal text. The new framework **separates administrative provisions from technical guidelines**, with the latter published as standalone documents by NIFDC (National Institutes for Food and Drug Control).

**Practical implications:**

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| Technical requirements fixed in regulation — slow to update | Technical guidelines updated independently — faster adaptation to industry needs |
| One monolithic dossier format | Modular dossier components aligned with technical guidance |
| Rigid submission structure | Flexible framework with clearer compliance boundaries |

For overseas buyers, this means **greater predictability** in regulatory timelines and **more transparent** compliance requirements when sourcing from suppliers who maintain current notification dossiers.

## What This Means for Overseas Buyers: A Decision Framework

### If You Are Sourcing Anti-Acne or Anti-Wrinkle Actives

This is the highest-impact change. Ingredients like ectoine, nicotinamide, GHK-Cu, and novel HA derivatives used in anti-acne and anti-wrinkle formulations now follow the **simplified notification pathway**.

| Factor | Old Pathway (Registration) | New Pathway (Notification) |
|--------|--------------------------|---------------------------|
| Review timeline | 12–24 months | 3–6 months |
| Data requirements | Full registration dossier | Streamlined notification package |
| Cost per ingredient | High | Reduced by ~50% |
| Supplier readiness | Limited suppliers with approved registration | More suppliers expected to enter |

**Action:** Prioritize suppliers who already have pre-assembled notification packages for these ingredient categories. The first movers gain 6–18 months of market exclusivity.

### If You Are Building Clean Beauty Supply Chains

The acceptance of alternative testing methods is a watershed moment for **clean beauty regulation compliance**. Ingredients sourced from suppliers with alternative testing data can now enter the market without the ethical and regulatory conflicts that animal testing creates for clean beauty brands.

**Action:** Request documentation of testing methodology from your ingredient suppliers. Suppliers who provide in vitro, computational, or read-across safety data — rather than animal study data — offer a cleaner compliance path for clean beauty positioning.

### If You Need Multi-Market Compliance

The convergence between NMPA's new framework and international standards (EU CosIng, FDA, REACH) means that **a single well-structured dossier** can now serve multiple regulatory jurisdictions more efficiently than before.

| Market | Key Framework | Overlap with New NMPA Rules |
|--------|--------------|---------------------------|
| EU | CosIng registration, REACH | Alternative testing alignment; safety data interchangeability |
| US | FDA food additive, OTC monograph | Safety assessment comparability |
| JP | MHLW quasi-drug | Functional claim categorization alignment |
| KR | MFDS functional cosmetic | Notification pathway similarity |

**Action:** Source from suppliers who proactively compile dossiers in multi-market format. This reduces duplicate testing and accelerates parallel market entry.

## FAQ

### Q1: Does the new regulation lower safety standards?
**A:** No. The 3-year post-market safety monitoring period remains mandatory for all new ingredients. The regulation streamlines *administrative* and *procedural* requirements — it does not reduce safety assessment rigor. During the 14th Five-Year Plan period, over 370 new ingredients were registered/notified with zero safety incidents.

### Q2: My ingredient was already registered under the 2021 rules. Do I need to re-register?
**A:** No. Existing registrations and notifications remain valid. The new provisions apply to applications filed after July 15, 2026.

### Q3: How does the alternative testing acceptance affect EU market access?
**A:** Positively. The alignment between NMPA's acceptance of alternative methods and the EU's long-standing animal testing ban means a single safety data package can now serve both markets more efficiently, reducing duplicate compliance costs.

### Q4: What is a "pre-assembled notification package"?
**A:** It's a complete set of documentation — safety assessment, stability data, impurity profile, manufacturing process description, and quality specifications — compiled in the format required by the NMPA Technical Guidelines. Suppliers who provide this eliminate the buyer's need to commission separate studies.

### Q5: How quickly can I get anti-acne ingredients to market under the new rules?
**A:** Under the notification pathway, typical timelines are 3–6 months from dossier submission to market access, compared to 12–24 months under the previous registration pathway. However, actual timelines depend on dossier completeness and regulatory workload.
