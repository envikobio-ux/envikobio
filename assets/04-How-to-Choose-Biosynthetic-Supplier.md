## The Problem: Different Stakeholders, Different Priorities

Selecting a biosynthetic ingredient supplier is never a single-decision-maker process. Three key roles drive the decision — and they care about fundamentally different things:

| Stakeholder | Top Priority | Typical Concern |
|------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **R&D Director / Brand CEO** | Innovation & compliance | Can this supplier support our clean beauty positioning? Do they have registration dossiers? Can they develop custom exclusive formulas? |
| **Procurement Manager / Distributor Owner** | Risk & reliability | Is pricing stable? Is MOQ flexible? Can I get samples fast? Is this a one-stop source? |
| **Production Head / Factory Director** | Operability & efficiency | Will this ingredient fit our existing production lines? What's the conversion rate? Is technology transfer included? |

Most supplier selection guides address only one of these perspectives. This guide addresses **all three** — because the right supplier must satisfy every stakeholder at the table.
